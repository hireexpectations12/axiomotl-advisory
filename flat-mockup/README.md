# Axiomotl flat / parallax mockup

Open `index.html` directly, or open the project-root `axiomotl-flat-mockup.html` for the portable version. The latter embeds every font, image, stylesheet and script. Rebuild it with `python flat-mockup/build.py` from the project root.

Slide the hero control or select **Align the pieces** to bring five scattered pieces into a connected method. Select any piece for an explanation; the same stage is selected in the method section. The method tabs support arrows, Home and End. Next stage wraps from Sustain to Analyse. Services use native disclosures. Email links open a draft addressed to hello@axiomotl.com.au; there is no contact-form backend.

GSAP drives the arrangement and pointer motion. ScrollTrigger moves the hero layers, statement band and method artwork at different speeds using native page scrolling. The motion switch disables parallax and animated transitions. The initial setting follows the operating system's reduced-motion preference. The slider and stage selection still work when motion is off, or when GSAP is unavailable. Without JavaScript the content, disclosures and contact remain readable; the remaining method stages have a static fallback.

Palette: acid yellow `#F2FF5A`, cobalt `#2145F5`, vermilion `#F45138`, ink `#171717`, paper `#F6F5EF`, white. Flat geometry, square controls, Manrope 400/600/800; no gradients or shadows.

The supplied logo and business facts come from the original user HTML. The logo has a monochrome CSS treatment. Fonts are reused from the local Google Fonts Manrope assets under the OFL. GSAP and ScrollTrigger 3.15.0 are stored locally from the official package distribution via jsDelivr; their license notices remain in the vendor directory and library headers. Motion follows the official [GSAP skills](https://github.com/greensock/gsap-skills) and [ScrollTrigger documentation](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/).

Previous spatial, copper and midnight/cyan alternatives remain separate.
