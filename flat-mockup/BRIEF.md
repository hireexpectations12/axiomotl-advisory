## Direction contract

A flat decision workshop: acid-yellow working surface, cobalt signals, vermilion interventions, paper and ink. Oversized Manrope 800, square controls and flat blocks, no gradients or shadows. The opening demonstration lets the visitor bring evidence, requirements, decisions, handover and ownership into an ordered path. A native range and one-click align/scatter control both operate the same model. Clickable pieces explain the corresponding method stage. The flat layers travel at different speeds on scroll; modest pointer response adds a direct connection to the graphic. Native page scrolling remains in control. No scroll hijacking, pinning or automatic carousel.

The first viewport must communicate business analysis, process governance and operational transition; visitors can explore four service disclosures, five method tabs, practitioner experience and a real email contact. No client outcomes or metrics are invented.
