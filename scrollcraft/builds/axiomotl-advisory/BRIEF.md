# Axiomotl Advisory redesign

Self-authored design decisions under the user's instruction to completely overhaul the website using the researched references and Scrollcraft. The supplied business description governs positioning; the earlier AI-marketplace premise is superseded.

## Supplied brief
“Axiomotl Advisory helps scientific and medical organisations improve how they work, manage change and prepare for everyday operations.”
“The focus is practical: clear decisions, defined responsibilities and processes your team knows how to follow.”

## Eight creative decisions
- Vibe: precise, assured, human, scientific. Mobbin principles: Twenty's concrete demonstration, Tines' task clarity, Retool's explicit responsibilities. No copied layouts.
- Journey: recognise the problem, understand the support, see a handover take shape, inspect actual work, meet the practice, start a conversation.
- Energy: composed opening, quiet service reading, one expressive working-example peak, calm evidence and contact.
- Feeling curve: recognition (specific scientific audience); relief (clear scope); clarity (an unresolved handover becomes assigned work); confidence (attributed source diagrams); familiarity (named practitioner); resolve (a direct email action).
- Signature: an illustrative handover worksheet progressively connects a requirement, accountable role and usable next step. Manual stage controls work independently of scrolling, with the selected topic carried into an email draft.
- Range: restrained professional editorial, with dimensional working documents rather than luminous technology imagery.
- Distinct scenes in natural document flow, with one bounded sticky working example on desktop. Mobile is unpinned.
- Assets: existing approved Axiomotl logo, supplied RSMS diagram excerpts, locally hosted Manrope and Newsreader. No fabricated photography, testimonials or performance numbers.

## Grammar: working dossier
A service website organised like an engagement dossier, not an application. Navigation is a compact in-flow contents bar. Sequence moves from situation to scope to working example to evidence to author to contact. The ending is an open correspondence panel. Ban full-screen video, decorative dashboards, repeated feature-card grids and scroll-gated essential text.
Why existing grammars lose: filmic and continuous world obstruct scanning; chaptered editorial's media-free opening loses the tangible working document; live surface implies a software product; typographic poster cannot accommodate source diagrams; gallery underexplains the service; split stage imposes a binary comparison; rhythmic cutlist is too energetic for the audience.

## Peak and layer contract
Peak: “It's the site where a messy handover becomes a clear plan with someone responsible for each next step.” The working example has the largest desktop scroll span (1.7 viewports), preceded by quiet service reading. No dead-scroll silence.
Hero: pale lilac far ground, fine diagram lines at the rear, an inclined requirement sheet in the middle, a white decision note in front. Two restrained independent parallax rates, stable semantic headline and controls. These are illustrative documents, explicitly labelled. Mobile puts the document composition below the heading and shortens travel. Reduced motion keeps the static layered composition.

## Score
| Beat | Device | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | Layered parallax | Make advisory outputs tangible |
| Relief | Flow + entrance | Let visitors scan support options |
| Clarity, peak | Sticky pin + bespoke worksheet | Connect requirement, responsibility and next step |
| Confidence | Broad image reveal | Reveal genuine working diagrams |
| Familiarity | Natural flow | Explain the practitioner's relevant background |
| Resolve | Static correspondence close | Offer one clear next action |
