Business & Governance Diagnostic

Discuss a diagnostic →

Find where work stalls, why the same issues return and which causes to address first.

When it helps

Handoffs keep breaking down. Teams rely on workarounds, or it is unclear who owns a decision. You need a shared account of what is happening before choosing a fix.

What the work involves

Map the current process with the people involved. Examine exceptions and control gaps, trace recurring problems to their causes, and agree which decisions need attention.

Outputs to agree in the scope

  • Current-state and stakeholder analysis
  • Root causes and control gaps
  • Prioritised decisions and actions

The diagnostic gives your team an evidence base for deciding what to tackle next.

Requirements & Decision Architecture

Discuss your requirements →

Turn operational and regulatory needs into requirements that can be traced to the decisions they support.

When it helps

Stakeholders describe the problem differently. Requirements are difficult to compare, or a procurement decision needs clearer evaluation criteria.

What the work involves

Clarify the needs with stakeholders and map how processes connect. Establish the evidence for each requirement, compare the options and make decision ownership explicit.

Outputs to agree in the scope

  • Requirements and traceability
  • Process, interface and decision maps
  • Procurement and evaluation artefacts

Keep the reasoning alongside the requirement, so the team can explain why it matters.

From project to practice

BAU Transition & Operating Model

Plan your transition →

Work out what must be ready before go-live, and how the receiving team will run and support the work afterwards.

When it helps

A project is nearing handover, but everyday responsibilities are still unclear. The receiving team needs a workable support model and material it can use.

What the work involves

Assess readiness for business-as-usual operations. Define roles and governance, work through support arrangements, and identify the training and handover material the team needs.

Outputs to agree in the scope

  • Readiness and transition assessment
  • Governance, roles and support model
  • Handover, training and assurance

Make the conditions for a usable handover clear before the project closes.

Senior support alongside your team

Embedded Principal BA Advisory

Discuss advisory support →

Bring an experienced business analyst into work that needs sustained attention across stakeholders and decisions.

When it helps

Your team needs senior analysis as the work develops, rather than a single standalone assessment. Stakeholder needs or vendor choices require ongoing scrutiny.

What the work involves

Work alongside your team on the agreed priorities. Support executive decisions, examine stakeholder and vendor questions, and review governance as circumstances change.

Outputs to agree in the scope

  • Executive decision support
  • Complex stakeholder and vendor analysis
  • Governance healthchecks and course correction

Agree the focus and responsibilities around the support your team actually needs.
