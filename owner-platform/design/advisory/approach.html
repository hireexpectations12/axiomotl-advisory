Working together

Agree the focus before the work begins.

You bring the context. Together, we agree the questions, the people to involve and the outputs the work needs to produce.

Proportionate to the problem

These stages guide the work; they do not prescribe a fixed programme. An engagement may focus on a diagnostic, a specific decision or preparation for handover.

Evidence people can use

Review findings with the people affected. Keep assumptions visible, record the reasoning behind decisions and leave material the team can work with.

01 / Analyse

Build a shared account of the problem.

What is happening, and what evidence supports that view?

Start with the work as it happens today. Bring together the people involved, the available evidence and the decisions that are proving difficult.

What we work through

Walk through the current process, including exceptions and workarounds. Identify where information changes hands, where controls are missing and who is affected. Test assumptions with the people doing the work.

What you take forward

An agreed problem statement, a current-state view and a prioritised set of questions to resolve.

02 / Design

Shape options that fit the operating context.

What would need to change for each option to work?

Translate the findings into practical requirements and possible ways forward. Consider how each option would affect people, processes, systems and governance.

What we work through

Map the proposed workflow and interfaces. Clarify the requirements and their rationale, then examine constraints, dependencies and trade-offs with stakeholders. Where procurement is involved, make evaluation criteria explicit.

What you take forward

Traceable requirements, workable options and criteria the team can use to compare them.

03 / Implement

Turn the selected direction into delivery.

What must happen, who owns it and what will Transition need?

Convert the agreed direction into sequenced delivery work. Keep owners, dependencies, risks and operational preparation visible.

What we work through

Confirm scope and acceptance criteria, sequence the work and assign owners. Track dependencies and unresolved risks, and involve the receiving team before handover planning begins.

What you take forward

A delivery plan with accountable owners, agreed controls and the inputs needed to prepare Transition.

04 / Transition

Prepare the receiving team for everyday operations.

Can the receiving team run and support the work?

A project handover needs more than a completion date. Establish what the receiving team will own and what must be ready before the work moves into business as usual.

What we work through

Check operational readiness with the teams involved. Define roles, support arrangements and escalation routes. Agree the training, documentation and assurance needed for a usable handover.

What you take forward

A transition plan, clear responsibilities and agreed readiness criteria, supported by handover material.

05 / Sustain

Review what happens after the change.

Is the change working, and what needs attention next?

Use evidence from everyday work to see whether the change is doing what was intended. Keep ownership and review arrangements clear as circumstances develop.

What we work through

Agree what to monitor and when to review it. Examine recurring exceptions and feedback with the responsible team, then decide whether processes, controls or support arrangements need adjustment.

What you take forward

Review points, named owners and a record of actions to address emerging issues.
