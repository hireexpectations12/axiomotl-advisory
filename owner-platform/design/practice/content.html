Our practice

Connect the evidence.
Make the next decision clear.

Axiomotl is a small, senior-led advisory practice focused on the practical work between an organisational ambition and a change that people can use. We help teams understand their current situation, agree what needs to change, and establish who will own the decisions and operations that follow.

That work brings together business analysis, process governance, requirements, procurement support and transition into business-as-usual. An engagement may begin with a specific question, a difficult handover, or a programme that needs a clearer basis for its next decision.

The aim is to leave the receiving team with usable evidence, clear responsibilities and documentation they can maintain.

Where that experience matters

Complexity often sits between teams, rather than within a single system.

Research and regulated environments

Scientific platforms bring together sample records, protocols, data access and reporting. We help make requirements, ownership and decision pathways explicit so that different groups can work to an agreed set of expectations.

Technology and vendor decisions

A product demonstration is only part of the evidence. We connect user needs, evaluation criteria, process dependencies and implementation constraints so a team can compare options and explain its decisions.

Transition into operations

Go-live is a milestone, not the end of the work. We help define readiness, support responsibilities, training and escalation paths so the receiving team knows what it owns and how to keep the service working.

How we work with you

Start with the actual work

Understand how people make decisions and move information today. Test assumptions against evidence, stakeholder conversations and the constraints of the organisation.

Make ownership visible

Agree who decides, who contributes and where exceptions go. Keep requirements, controls and decisions connected so the reasoning stays understandable.

Build for the receiving team

Make outputs useful beyond the engagement. Process maps, decision records, readiness checks and handover materials should support the people responsible for the next step.
